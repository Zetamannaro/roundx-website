# Third-party assets
## React Bits
Grainient.tsx/css, AcidSquares.tsx/css (retained but unused) and CircularGallery.tsx/css by David Haz:
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits
Retrieved 2026-11-12. MIT + Commons Clause. Full text: licenses/react-bits-LICENSE.md.
CircularGallery is adapted for below-image country/industry captions, external website links, accessible anchors and normal vertical scrolling. Grainient uses the original source; angle and flow are driven through props by page scrolling.
Included in the RoundX website, not distributed as a standalone component library.

## Poppins
Bundled locally through @fontsource/poppins under SIL Open Font License.
See licenses/poppins-OFL.txt.

## Map
World map paths from @svg-maps/world, based on MapSVG: https://mapsvg.com/maps/world (CC BY 4.0).
See licenses/svg-maps-world-LICENSE.md. Coloring and interactive highlights adapted for RoundX.

## Company portfolio logos
Company logos are retrieved from official websites, official linked profiles or the user's existing RoundX portfolio. See PORTFOLIO_ASSETS.md for per-asset URLs and native-vector vs embedded-raster distinctions. Original brand colors are preserved. Logos identify the portfolio list supplied by the user and remain the property of their owners.
