# Portfolio logo provenance

24 entities and all geographies, sectors and outcomes are supplied by RoundX. Multi-country companies are assigned to each specified country but counted once in company totals.

All 24 logo SVGs contain scalable vector geometry, with transparent backgrounds. No raster images, image patterns, external fonts or base64 artwork remain. Ten use native official vector geometry (including TextYess rearranged to the supplied profile variant and GemaTEG/TimeFlow restored to original full-color references); fourteen are reconstructed vector paths from original raster references.

Vector redraws preserve source shapes and brand colors but are reconstructions, not original brand master files. Raster references cannot reveal detail absent from their source. Small historical microtext, especially XWorks, retains source-quality limits.

Up2You, Cents, Evenfi and TextYess were checked against the exact references supplied in chat. Cents and TextYess retain their white lettering; the purple profile tiles were removed. Official white logo elements elsewhere are preserved without adding dark backplates.

The WebGL gallery rasterizes the SVG geometry at a 1536px longest edge for texture sampling and renders at 2× pixel density, rather than uploading low intrinsic SVG dimensions. The non-WebGL fallback displays the original SVGs directly.
