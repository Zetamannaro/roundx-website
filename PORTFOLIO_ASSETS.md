## Identity checks

Cents is the Italian donations fintech, not the US laundry company.

This historical logo is preferred to an unverified newer-domain rebrand. White background alpha is removed in the SVG filter; the colored artwork is retained. Source identities do not override the outcomes supplied by RoundX.
Closed-company domains can be unavailable; Cents links to its official documentation.
